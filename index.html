What really controls a small weir?

A small weir is easy to draw as a section. It is harder to understand as a system. Change discharge, crest length or height and the effect can travel through head, unit discharge, jump behaviour, material demand, stability and the foundation question. This article follows those links using the study’s synthetic evidence.

A small weir looks simple. Its dependencies are not.

This is not a search for one “best” section. It is a sequence of questions about which variables matter, under what conditions, and where the evidence becomes uncertain. Each chapter starts with the evidence, then separates interpretation from limitation.

Research boundary. This is a synthetic research article, not a design calculator or construction guide. It can clarify relationships and trade-offs, but final field design still requires site-specific hydrology, foundation evidence, applicable standards and professional engineering review.
